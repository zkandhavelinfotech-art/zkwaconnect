Complete Login & Authorization Demonstration Flow

1

Start Logged-Out (Show Address Bar)

Open an Incognito / Private window and navigate to https://app.zkwaconnect.in/login. Ensure the browser URL bar is clearly visible. The app shows the logged-out state with the native sign-in form.

2

Log In with Native Email & Password (No Facebook Login)

Enter admin@zkwaconnect.in and admin2026! into the login fields. Click Sign In. The user lands directly in the ZKWAConnect dashboard / project workspace.

3

Navigate to Meta Connect Step

Inside the app, open Settings → Meta Integration or go to /onboarding/meta-connect. The user sees the "Log in to connect your Meta business" section featuring the official "Log in with Facebook" button and WhatsApp Embedded Signup card.

4

Click "Log in with Facebook" & Grant Permissions

Click the blue "Log in with Facebook" button. The Meta OAuth popup dialog opens:

  • User logs in with their Facebook account credentials.
  • Selects the Meta Business Portfolio.
  • Selects the WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) and phone number.
  • Grants the requested permissions: whatsapp_business_management, whatsapp_business_messaging, and business_management.
  • Clicks Done / Continue. The popup automatically closes.
5

Demonstrate Data Usage in the App

ZKWAConnect receives the token securely and immediately demonstrates functional data usage:

  • WABA Listing: Lists all synced WhatsApp Business Accounts and Phone Numbers (/onboarding/waba-setup).
  • Message Templates: Fetches and displays Meta message templates (/templates).
  • Team Inbox: Demonstrates sending and receiving real-time WhatsApp messages (/inbox).

Permissions & Features Justification

whatsapp_business_management Feature: WABA & Template Management

Purpose: Allows registered businesses to view their WhatsApp Business Account ID, verified phone numbers, quality ratings, and manage message templates directly within ZKWAConnect without needing to navigate Meta Business Manager.

whatsapp_business_messaging Feature: Real-Time Messaging & Inbox

Purpose: Enables customer service teams to receive inbound customer inquiries and send responses, transactional notifications, and broadcast messages via the WhatsApp Business API.

business_management Feature: Portfolio Integration

Purpose: Allows the app to list the user's business portfolios to correctly associate the selected WABA with the user's tenant account in ZKWAConnect.
