🔵 Step 0 · Meta Developer App

Create your Meta Developer App

Before connecting WhatsApp, you need a Meta Developer App. This takes 5 minutes and only needs to be done once.

One-time setupMeta API v20.0App ID + SecretWebhook ready
🔵
What this does: You need a Meta Developer App to use the WhatsApp Business API. This page guides you through creating one at developers.facebook.com — ZKWAConnect will then link to it using your App ID and App Secret.
1
Log in to Meta Developer Portal
Go to developers.facebook.com and log in with the Facebook account connected to your Meta Business Manager.
Open Meta Developer Portal →
2
Click "My Apps" → "Create App"
In the top-right navigation click My Apps, then click the green Create App button.
📍 developers.facebook.com → My Apps → Create App
3
Choose use case: "Other"
When asked "What do you want your app to do?" — select Other. This gives you access to all product APIs including WhatsApp Business.
🛍️
Consumer
🏢
Business
⚙️
Other ✓
Select this
4
Choose app type: "Business"
On the next screen select Business as the app type. This enables access to the WhatsApp Business API and Business Management APIs.
Business app type WhatsApp API access
5
Fill in app details
Enter your app name and contact email. Select your Meta Business Manager from the dropdown. These are used for your developer account — not shown to WhatsApp users.
App Display Name
ZKWAConnect Business
Contact Email
you@yourbusiness.in
6
Add WhatsApp product to your app
After the app is created, you land on the App Dashboard. Click Add Product, find WhatsApp and click Set up.
App Dashboard → Add Product → WhatsApp → Set up
7
Copy App ID and App Secret
Go to Settings → Basic. Copy your App ID and App Secret. You will paste these into ZKWAConnect Settings → WABA Configuration.
META_APP_ID
1234567890123456

••••••••••••••••
⚠️
Never share your App Secret publicly. Do not commit it to GitHub. Store it only in your .env.production file.
8
Paste credentials into ZKWAConnect
Go to ZKWAConnect → Settings → WABA Configuration → paste your App ID and App Secret. Then proceed to the WhatsApp Setup wizard to create your WABA and register your phone number.
Open Settings → WhatsApp Setup Wizard

Development vs Live mode
🧪 Development mode (default)
Can only message test numbers added to your app. Good for development and staging testing.
🚀 Live mode (after approval)
Can message any opted-in WhatsApp number. Requires Meta app review. Apply at Meta App Dashboard → App Review.
