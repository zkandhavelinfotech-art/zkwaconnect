Connect Facebook Account
Link your Meta Business Manager to enable WhatsApp Business API
ℹ️
You need a Meta Business Manager account and a verified business to use the WhatsApp Business API. Personal Facebook accounts cannot be used.
1
Click "Login with Facebook" below
You will be redirected to Meta's secure OAuth page. ZKWAConnect uses the official Meta Business Login flow — we never store your Facebook password.
2
Grant required permissions
Accept the following permissions when Meta prompts: whatsapp_business_management, whatsapp_business_messaging, business_management. All three are required for ZKWAConnect to work.
whatsapp_business_management whatsapp_business_messaging business_management
3
Select your Business Manager
If you have multiple Meta Business Managers, select the one that owns your WhatsApp Business Account (WABA). If you don't have a WABA yet, you can create one in the next step.
4
Authorize ZKWAConnect
Click "Continue" to complete OAuth. You will be returned to ZKWAConnect automatically. Your access token is encrypted and stored securely — it is never exposed in the UI.
5
Complete WhatsApp setup
After login, you will be redirected to WhatsApp Setup to create your WABA and register your phone number.
