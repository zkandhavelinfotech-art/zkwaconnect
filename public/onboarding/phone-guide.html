Register your WhatsApp phone number

Verify and activate your business phone number on WhatsApp Business API. OTP verification via SMS or voice call.

Before you begin: The phone number must not be registered on any WhatsApp account (personal or Business App). If it is, delete that account first via WhatsApp app → Settings → Account → Delete account.
1
Select your WABA account
Choose the WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) you created in the previous step. Your phone number will be registered under this WABA.
2
Enter your phone number
Enter the full number with country code (e.g. +91 99876 54321). Supported: Mobile numbers, landlines (with voice OTP), VoIP numbers (Twilio, etc.).
3
Choose verification method
Meta will send a 6-digit OTP via SMS or Voice call. Select the method that works for your number type.
SMS OTP Voice OTP
4
Enter 6-digit OTP
Enter the code received. OTP is valid for 10 minutes. If not received after 60 seconds, click "Resend".
5
Number activated ✅
Your phone number is now registered on the WhatsApp Business API. It will appear in ZKWAConnect Settings → WABA Accounts with status Active.
